# P6.1 Adoption and Hardening Review

**Status:** PASS — bounded follow-on slice
**Date:** 2026-07-27
**Decision:** P6.1 proves authoring readiness, bounded resource handling,
interrupted-enable recovery, and deterministic multi-repository adoption
without opening P7 or claiming production readiness.

## Objective

Strengthen the completed P6 declarative-local ecosystem with evidence that:

- a new package author has a usable schema, template, validation command, and
  safety guide;
- oversized or malformed local inputs fail before expensive parsing;
- a retained immutable manifest snapshot can recover after lifecycle writes
  are interrupted;
- reference extensions produce deterministic normalized results on multiple
  fixed repository snapshots; and
- pilot execution does not modify the source repositories.

## Resource and recovery hardening

- Extension manifests larger than 256 KiB fail with
  `AOS-EXTENSION-MANIFEST-TOO-LARGE`.
- Rust adapter inputs larger than 1 MiB fail with
  `AOS-EXTENSION-ADAPTER-INPUT-TOO-LARGE`.
- Invalid UTF-8 fails as a manifest read error without lifecycle mutation.
- Matching retained manifest snapshots are reused after an interrupted enable;
  validated and enabled revisions are appended without overwriting the
  snapshot.
- Conflicting retained snapshots continue to fail with immutable ownership
  conflict.

These tests demonstrate bounded local recovery. They do not claim a
multi-record transaction, distributed lock, or crash-proof filesystem.
